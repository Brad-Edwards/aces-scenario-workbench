Privacy

The workbench stores the account data you provide (email address and optional display name) and the review activity you create (comments, decisions, review-state changes, and membership records). This data is used only to operate the review workspace.

You can download a copy of your personal data or delete your account at any time from your account page. Deleting your account removes your account record and the comments and decisions you authored. Activity-stream entries are retained with your identity removed.

Scenario content itself originates from ACES scenario packs and is governed by those packs, not by the workbench.
